# Russian frequency word list attribution

The expanded starter vocabulary includes data derived from the University of Leeds Russian Internet Corpus, cleaned and published by the `hingston/russian` project. The source repository distributes the data under a Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) license.

Source: `https://github.com/hingston/russian`
Included source file: `10000-russian-words-cyrillic-only.txt`
Corpus editing and cleanup: hingston

The list was normalized (`ё` → `е`), deduplicated, and merged with the original Local Type Assist starter vocabulary.
